## The problem

A heating and boiler company was winning work entirely through word of mouth. The phone rang
when it rang, which meant no visibility, no record of enquiries, and no way to know which
services people were actually looking for.

They needed enquiries to arrive on their own, at any hour, without staff answering a phone — and
they needed the site to be findable by people searching locally.

## What I built

A service website built around the jobs they actually want booked, not around company history.

**Online booking** — customers schedule a heating or boiler appointment directly, so an enquiry
becomes a booked job rather than a missed call.

**Dedicated service pages** — separate pages for boiler installation, repair and maintenance,
each targeting the specific search a customer makes at the moment of need.

**Quote and contact forms** — built-in enquiry forms with server-side validation, so submissions
are usable and spam is filtered before it reaches the inbox.

**Mobile-first responsive design** — most local searches happen on a phone, often while standing
in a cold house, so the booking path is the shortest path on the site.

**SEO foundation** — structured page content, service-specific pages, local signals and clean
metadata, so the site is discoverable for the searches that convert.

## Tech stack

| Layer | Choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CMS | WordPress | The business needs to edit service pages and pricing themselves without a developer, and the local search results for this trade reward a properly structured content site |
| Front-end | JavaScript | Progressive enhancement for the booking flow and form validation |
| SEO | Technical on-page SEO | Structured data, per-service metadata and fast load times on mobile |

## Challenges and learnings

The main challenge was making booking reliable while keeping site editing simple. The first
version coupled the booking flow tightly to the CMS, which made both harder to maintain — editing
content risked breaking submissions, and changing the booking logic required touching templates.
Rebuilding it as a self-contained flow, with the CMS responsible only for content, fixed that and
made the whole site far easier for the business to run.

Contact form spam was the other recurring issue. Relying on a single honeypot field was not
enough, so validation, rate limiting and a challenge step were combined. Volume dropped sharply
without losing genuine enquiries.

Performance work mattered more than expected. On mobile connections the difference between a
heavily themed page and a stripped-back one is the difference between a booking and a bounce.

## Outcome

Delivered in **November 2024**. Enquiries now arrive directly and are scheduled rather than
played back over the phone, the business can update its own service pages, and the site is
visible for the local searches that drive emergency and installation work.
